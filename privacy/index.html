Privacy Policy — OpenLine AI

Last updated: November 2026

OpenLine AI is a Chrome extension. It does not operate its own servers and does not collect, store, or transmit your data to us in any form.

What the extension does

  • When you click “Generate opener,” the extension reads the visible text of the webpage in your active browser tab.
  • That text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (OpenAI or Anthropic), using the API key you provided.
  • The generated opener is shown to you in the extension popup and is not stored by us.

What is stored, and where

  • Your API key, chosen provider, optional “about me” text and license key are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never sent to any server operated by OpenLine AI.
  • Daily usage counts for the free tier are stored the same way, locally only.

Third parties

  • Page content and generation requests go to OpenAI (openai.com) or Anthropic (anthropic.com), depending on your choice, and are governed by their privacy policies.
  • License key verification requests go to Gumroad (gumroad.com), governed by its privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We don’t use your data for advertising, analytics or any purpose unrelated to generating openers.
  • We don’t use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
